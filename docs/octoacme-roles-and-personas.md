# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analysts or Requirements Leads

### Role Summary
Business Analysts or Requirements Leads elicit, analyze, and maintain requirements, connecting stakeholder and user needs to product priorities and testable delivery criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business, user, and system requirements
- Maintain traceability as requirements and scope change
- Clarify assumptions, constraints, dependencies, and requirement gaps
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to make requirements testable and clarify acceptance criteria
- Coordinate with UX/UI Designers or User Researchers on user needs and experience requirements

### Goals
- Give the team a shared understanding of the problem and expected outcomes
- Reduce missed requirements, ambiguity, and avoidable rework
- Keep requirements aligned with product priorities and agreed scope

### Typical Communication
- Requirements and discovery workshops
- Backlog refinement and acceptance-criteria discussions
- Requirement updates, traceability records, and decision logs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to connect stakeholder needs to product outcomes and priorities
- Works with Project Managers to clarify scope, dependencies, and planning impacts
- Coordinates with UX/UI Designers or User Researchers on user needs and experience requirements
- Collaborates with Developers and QA/Testing to confirm feasibility, testability, and acceptance criteria

---

## Data or Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data or Analytics Leads define how teams will measure product outcomes and ensure the data needed to evaluate those outcomes is collected and trustworthy.

### Responsibilities
- Translate agreed success metrics into an instrumentation and measurement plan
- Define event tracking, data quality expectations, dashboards, and reporting needs
- Coordinate instrumentation with Developers and validate the resulting data
- Review post-release measurements and communicate findings and limitations

### Goals
- Make product outcomes measurable and interpretable
- Identify instrumentation or data quality gaps before they undermine evaluation
- Help teams use evidence to inform product decisions and improvements

### Typical Communication
- Metric-definition and instrumentation planning
- Data validation and dashboard reviews
- Post-release outcome readouts and recommendations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns with Product Managers on success metrics, outcome definitions, and how findings inform priorities
- Works with Developers to plan and implement instrumentation
- Partners with QA/Testing to validate event behavior, data quality, and reporting
- Shares measurement needs and post-release findings with Project Managers and stakeholders

---

## Change Management or Training Leads

### Role Summary
Change Management or Training Leads prepare affected users and teams to adopt a change through timely communications, training, and enablement.

### Responsibilities
- Assess who is affected by a change and what support they will need
- Prepare training, enablement materials, and adoption communications
- Coordinate communications and enablement with project and release plans
- Gather adoption feedback and identify follow-up support or training needs

### Goals
- Help affected users understand and adopt changes
- Prepare support teams to respond to questions and issues
- Reduce disruption and confusion during rollout

### Typical Communication
- Change-impact and stakeholder-readiness discussions
- Training sessions, release communications, and enablement materials
- Adoption feedback and follow-up readiness updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinates timing, stakeholder communications, and dependencies with Project Managers
- Aligns messaging and expected user outcomes with Product Managers
- Works with Delivery or Release Managers on launch timing and readiness
- Prepares Customer Support or Operations Representatives with training, documentation, and escalation guidance

---

## Role Interaction Model

These personas describe responsibilities, not necessarily separate positions. Teams can combine or tailor roles to project size, but should assign a clear owner for each responsibility during planning and agree on the artifacts and handoffs expected.

- Business Analyst or Requirements Lead, Product Manager, Developers, and QA/Testing: connect stakeholder needs and product priorities to traceable requirements and testable acceptance criteria.
- Data or Analytics Lead, Product Manager, Developers, and QA/Testing: turn success metrics into instrumentation and validate measurement so post-release outcomes can be assessed.
- Change Management or Training Lead, Project Manager, Product Manager, Delivery or Release Manager, and Customer Support or Operations Representative: coordinate communications, enablement, launch readiness, and support handoff.

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- For small teams, multiple responsibilities may be combined in a single person; make ownership and handoffs explicit during planning.
