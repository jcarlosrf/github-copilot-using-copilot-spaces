# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme's project-management guidance. The documents work together to help teams validate initiatives, plan and deliver work, communicate progress, manage risk, release safely, and improve from each project cycle.

## OctoAcme project management processes

OctoAcme's project lifecycle begins with **initiation**, where the team validates the business need, identifies stakeholders and success measures, and decides whether to proceed. Approved work moves into **planning**, where the team defines scope, prioritizes and estimates the backlog, identifies dependencies, and agrees on milestones and a Definition of Done.

During **execution**, teams deliver in small increments, track work on a project board, check quality, and communicate progress and blockers. **Release and deployment** activities confirm readiness, validate changes, and prepare verification and rollback plans. After a release or milestone, the team **closes out and reflects** through a retrospective, recording follow-up actions to support continuous improvement.

Throughout the lifecycle, OctoAcme emphasizes clear roles and ownership, proactive risk and dependency management, regular stakeholder communication, and appropriate testing and quality checks.
